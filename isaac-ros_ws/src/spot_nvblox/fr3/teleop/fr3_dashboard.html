      What the browser sees / troubleshooting 
      
        
      The browser only lists a pad after you press a button while this tab is focused (click the page first). Close other tabs or tester pages that may hold the pad, and quit Steam / DS4Windows / Remote Play if they are running (they take the pad over). PS4 over USB or Bluetooth both work in Chrome/Edge; Firefox may report a non-standard mapping, which is refused.
      
      
      
      
      PS4: Options = engage, Share = disengage, left stick = x/y, right stick = roll/pitch, L1/L2 = up/down, R1/R2 = yaw, Cross/Circle = close/open gripper.
