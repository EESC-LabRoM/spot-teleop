# Palm 6D stabilization — 23 October 2026

Installed in the existing MediaPipe Hands perception path. Home, the ROS robot backend, workspace, velocities and gripper confirmation were not modified. The arm was disengaged for the perception restart; no motion test was issued.

- A latched per-person five-point palm template is fitted with proper rigid rotations. The wrist and at least three MCPs must agree; one inconsistent MCP may be excluded. Degenerate collinear support or inconsistent scale is rejected. Current measurements supply every fit; the template never extrapolates hand motion. Fit residual and inlier count are published in diagnostics.
- An adaptive quaternion filter smooths the optical-frame orientation. An isolated large flip is rejected; returning after a long gap requires two consistent current measurements. Short large changes require three agreeing samples and retain the angular speed cap. Quaternion sign changes do not count as physical rotations.
- The wrist TF and displayed axes now use filtered measured camera position/orientation. Previously the robot input was filtered while this TF used the raw palm frame. The gripper orientation clutch remains on the control path only; it is not represented as a physical palm measurement. An extra position smoothing stage was not added: the camera TF position is obtained by inverting the existing rotation and latched scale of the filtered control input.
- Depth uses current wrist/MCP sites plus four interior-palm sites. Consistent interior readings can recover current depth when silhouette sites have holes. No historical depth is substituted.
- RGB-D exact synchronization queue increased from two to six, retaining the latest-only inference worker and unchanged input/output age limits. This accommodates callback skew without making old frames valid.
- Normal Start retains the current operator association as well as measured arm scale. Hand identity may bridge up to five seconds within the existing spatial gate; this matches the recovery window. Force/new-person calibration clears it and the palm template. Missing current pose data still stops control.

45 pure tests passed: rigid rotations/translations, a bad knuckle, collinear rejection, quaternion sign invariance, transient flips, re-acquisition, stationary jitter reduction, bounded continuous rotation, current interior depth support, plus previous gesture/calibration tests. These are synthetic verifications, not a live precision benchmark. During initial inspection the working hand was frequently at the frame edge or occluded by the desk. No filter can establish an observed 6D pose for a fully hidden hand; camera framing remains necessary.
